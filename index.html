The Day

Built for the students who needed it most

Students’ Day 2016 was CNU’s answer to a rough exam season — a day to trade blue books for balloons. Drum and bugle corps opened the gates at seven, the Doxology and Anthem grounded the morning, and by 8:30 the booths were open and Larong Pinoy was underway: Palosebo, Sack Race with a Twist, Kadang-Kadang, and more, all played out under a sky that stayed stubbornly cloudy and never once got in the way.

“Cloudy, mild, and completely unbothered — the weather that let the whole fiesta happen.”

Larong Pinoy

Six games, one very sore next morning

The heart of the morning program — classic Filipino street games, campus-ified.

🎪

Palosebo

A greased bamboo pole, a prize at the top, and a crowd cheering on every failed attempt.

🛍️

Sack Race with a Twist

The classic hop-in-a-sack sprint, with a CNU-only twist nobody saw coming.

🥥

Kadang-Kadang

Coconut-shell stilts and a whole lot of wobbling toward the finish line.

🌾

Sinsilyo sa Harina

Coins buried in a mound of flour — dig in face-first, come out dusted white.

🍳

Sinsilyo sa Kawali

Same coin hunt, this time through a pan of something a lot less appetizing.

🏁

Amazing Race

A campus-wide dash through checkpoints, closing out the morning at a sprint.
